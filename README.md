## Project Description

The Diabetes Prediction Analysis Project focuses on exploring patient health data to identify factors that contribute to diabetes outcomes. The project uses data analysis and visualization techniques to examine patterns in patient health metrics such as glucose level, BMI, age, blood pressure, insulin, and pregnancies.      

The goal of the project is to provide meaningful insights that can help understand diabetes risk factors and support data-driven healthcare decisions. An interactive dashboard was created using Microsoft Power BI to visualize trends, comparisons, and key performance indicators.

## Business Problems

Diabetes is a major health challenge affecting millions of people worldwide. Detecting diabetes early can help patients get proper treatment and reduce health risks. Healthcare professionals need to understand how factors like glucose level, BMI, age, and blood pressure affect diabetes in patients.    

This project analyzes patient health data to identify patterns and trends related to diabetes and presents the findings using charts, reports, and interactive dashboard.


## Project Objectives

- Analyze factors affecting diabetes prediction
- Identify patterns in patient demographics and health metrics
- Compare diabetic and non-diabetic patients
- Analyze patient health data to identify factors contributing to diabetes
- Build an interactive Microsoft Power BI dashboard for data-driven insights

  ## Dataset Information
  The dataset contains patient medical information used for diabetes prediction analysis.

Key Columns:
- Pregnancies
- Glucose
- Blood Pressure
- Skin Thickness
- Insulin
- BMI
- Diabetes Pedigree Function
- Age
- Outcome (Diabetic or Non-Diabetic)

## Tools Used 
- Microsoft power BI

 Power BI Features Used 
- Power Query (for data cleaning and transformation)
- Data Modeling (creating relationships between tables)
- DAX (for calculated measures and KPIs)
- Visualizations (bar charts, pie charts, line charts, cards, etc.)

## Data Cleaning Process 
- Checked for missing values
- Removed duplicates
- Corrected invalid or zero values in medical columns
- Converted data types where necessary
- Prepared data for visualization and analysis

  ## Analysis Performed
Patient Demographics Analysis
- Age distribution of patients
- Pregnancy count analysis
- BMI category comparison

Health Metrics Analysis
- Glucose level analysis
- Blood pressure comparison
- Insulin and BMI trends

Diabetes Outcome Analysis
- Diabetic vs non-diabetic patient comparison
- Relationship between glucose level and diabetes outcome
- Impact of age and BMI on diabetes prediction

## Dahboard Features
-  The interactive dashboard includes
- Total Patients
- Total Diabetic Patients
- Average Glucose Level
- Average BMI
- Age Group Analysis
- Diabetes Outcome Distribution
- Health Metric Comparisons
- Interactive Filters and Slicers  
Built using Microsoft Power BI for easy exploration of insights.

## Key Insights 
- Majority of patients are non-diabetic (65.1%)
- Diabetes rate is approximately 34.9%
- Higher glucose levels are strongly linked to diabetes
- Patients with higher BMI show more diabetes cases 
- Increase in pregnancies slightly increases diabetes risk
- Middle-aged individuals have higher diabetes prevalence

## Recommendations
- Encourage routine medical checkups for patients at higher risk of diabetes
- Promote healthy eating, regular exercise, and better lifestyle habits to help reduce diabetes risk
-  Give more preventive attention to patients with high glucose and BMI levels
-  Use data analysis and prediction methods to support early diagnosis and proper treatment of diabetes

## Conclusion 
This project provides valuable insights into diabetes prediction using patient health data. By analyzing important health metrics and visualizing findings through an interactive dashboard, the project helps improve understanding of diabetes risk factors and supports better healthcare decision-making.
